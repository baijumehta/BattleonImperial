Dear Community Partner,

We are excited to invite you to be a sponsor of the inaugural Battle on Imperial Girls Lacrosse Tournament, happening Saturday, March 13, 2027, at Canyon High School in Anaheim Hills, California.

Battle on Imperial brings together competitive girls lacrosse programs from across Southern California for a full day of high-level competition, community spirit and a premier event experience — varsity and JV, three full-size fields, one campus. With 500+ players, coaches and families expected, the tournament is an incredible opportunity to showcase your business while supporting youth sports and our local community.

Why become a sponsor?

  • High-visibility exposure to local and regional families, athletes and coaches
  • Support the growth of girls lacrosse and youth sports in our community
  • Align your brand with a positive, active and community-focused event
  • Reach a targeted audience through event signage, social media, digital marketing and more
  • Be part of an event that promotes leadership, teamwork and healthy lifestyles

Your support makes a difference

Battle on Imperial is put on by the Canyon High School Lacrosse Booster Club, a volunteer parent organization. Sponsorships pay for the parts of the day that make it a first-class event for every team that comes — field preparation, certified officials, athletic training, player packets, shade and water at every field. Your support keeps entry affordable for the programs that play, keeps the day free for the families who watch, and backs girls lacrosse at Canyon High School.

Together, we can make an impact

We would be honored to partner with you for this exciting event. The packages on the first page are a starting point — our team is happy to customize one to meet your business goals, and in-kind support (water, ice, food, swag) is just as welcome.

Thank you for considering supporting Battle on Imperial. We hope you will join us in making this tournament a memorable and successful event for our players, families and community.

With gratitude,
