so check the bio before you follow.

gives you most of the rest in a couple of minutes.

## Working the list

**Pace it.** A brand-new account that follows 200 people on day one gets action-
blocked. Roughly 10–20 a day for the first fortnight, then up to about 50.

**Follow with intent.** Like a post or two from each account as you go. A bare
follow from an account with no posts reads as a bot.

**Comment before you ask for anything.** A genuine comment on a team's game post
does more than fifty follows. These are the coaches and parents who decide
whether to enter.

**Post before you follow.** Have the announcement graphic and three or four
posts up first — an empty account converts nobody.

**Priority order:** the Rose Bowl nine, then `@noclax`, then North OC schools,
then the clubs, then wider OC.
