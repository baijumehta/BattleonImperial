# Outreach drafts

Three short asks, written to be sent as-is by Lydie. Each one exists to get a
link to battleonimperial.com from somewhere Google already trusts — that, far
more than anything on the site itself, is what makes a new domain rank.

Facts used: Saturday, March 13, 2027 · Canyon High School, Anaheim Hills ·
girls varsity and JV · CIF Southern Section Division 2 and 3 · three fields,
one campus · three guaranteed varsity games · free for spectators, free
parking · early bird entry through November 30 · Lydie Gutfeld, 714-747-4770,
battleonimperial@gmail.com. No team count, no "proceeds".

---

## 1. Link request — Canyon High School website

Send to whoever edits the Canyon athletics pages (athletic director or the
district web contact). A link from a school site is the strongest single
signal a new tournament site can get.

> **Subject:** Link for the Battle on Imperial lacrosse tournament
>
> Hi [name],
>
> The Canyon High School Lacrosse Booster Club is hosting **Battle on
> Imperial**, a one-day girls high school lacrosse tournament, on Saturday,
> March 13, 2027, here on campus. Varsity and JV programs from across Southern
> California will play on three fields, and it's free for spectators.
>
> Could you add a link to the tournament site from the Girls Lacrosse page
> under Athletics (and the school news feed, if there's room)? Suggested text:
>
> > **Battle on Imperial — Girls Lacrosse Tournament · March 13, 2027**
> > Canyon hosts a one-day varsity and JV tournament on campus. Free for
> > spectators. Details and entry: https://www.battleonimperial.com
>
> The site has the schedule, campus map and parking information, so linking
> to it will also save the office some phone calls in March.
>
> Thank you,
> Lydie Gutfeld
> Event Organizer, Battle on Imperial · Canyon High School Lacrosse Booster Club
> 714-747-4770 · battleonimperial@gmail.com

The same note, with "your league page" in place of "the Girls Lacrosse page",
goes to the Orange County girls lacrosse league and to USA Lacrosse's events
calendar (they take submissions through their site).

---

## 2. Entry-packet paragraph — for every team that enters

Goes at the end of the entry packet, after the rules. Coaches will do this if
it's one sentence to copy; they won't if it's a favour to think about.

> **Help us spread the word**
>
> Once your spot is confirmed, we'd be grateful if your athletics office or
> team account posted that you're playing. It helps families find the
> schedule and helps the tournament grow. Copy and paste is fine:
>
> > *[School] girls lacrosse will compete at Battle on Imperial, a one-day
> > tournament at Canyon High School in Anaheim Hills on Saturday, March 13,
> > 2027. Schedule, map and parking: https://www.battleonimperial.com*
>
> On Instagram, tag **@battleonimperial** and we'll share it. The schedule
> page has a **Copy link** button that opens straight on your team — handy
> for the parent group chat.

---

## 3. Press blurb — local news and newsletters

For Anaheim Hills News, the OC Register preps desk, the Orange Unified School
District newsletter, and any school or club newsletter that will run it. Short
enough to print unedited. The quote is a suggestion for Lydie to approve or
replace.

> **Anaheim Hills to host its first girls lacrosse tournament**
>
> Canyon High School will host **Battle on Imperial**, a one-day girls high
> school lacrosse tournament, on Saturday, March 13, 2027. Varsity and junior
> varsity teams from across Southern California will play round-robin pools
> on three fields on the Canyon campus, with every varsity team guaranteed
> three games. Admission and parking are free.
>
> The tournament is organized by the Canyon High School Lacrosse Booster
> Club, a volunteer parent group. "Orange County programs have spent years
> driving to Los Angeles County for a tournament day," said organizer Lydie
> Gutfeld. "We wanted one that families could get to in twenty minutes and
> watch all day."
>
> Teams can enter at battleonimperial.com, where early entry pricing runs
> through November 30. Local businesses interested in sponsoring can find
> packages on the same site.
>
> **Contact:** Lydie Gutfeld, 714-747-4770, battleonimperial@gmail.com
> **Web:** https://www.battleonimperial.com · Instagram @battleonimperial

---

## After sending

- Ask for the link to be `https://www.battleonimperial.com` (with www), which
  is the canonical address; the apex redirects there anyway.
- Search Console → Links shows which of these landed, usually a few weeks
  later. Nothing to do when it does.
