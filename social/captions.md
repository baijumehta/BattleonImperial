## Posting notes

- **Portrait (1080x1350) beats square** for feed reach. Default to it.
- Post the same graphic to Stories with a link sticker once you have the
  domain, since Stories links convert better than "link in bio".
- Best times for this audience are early evening on weekdays, and Saturday
  mornings during season when parents are already at fields.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. It is the cheapest reach there is.
- Do not post all nine of these in a week. One or two a week until the date
  locks, then build toward registration.
