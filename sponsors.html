Why Become a Sponsor

A Full Day, A Captive Crowd

  • High-visibility exposure to local and regional families, athletes and coaches — on the field, on the PA, on this site and on social media.
  • Support the growth of girls lacrosse and youth sports in our community.
  • Align your brand with a positive, active, community-focused event.
  • Reach a targeted audience through event signage, social media, digital marketing and more.
  • Be part of an event that promotes leadership, teamwork and healthy lifestyles.

Your Support Makes a Difference

Battle on Imperial is put on by the Canyon High School Lacrosse Booster Club — a volunteer parent organization. Sponsorships pay for the parts of the day that make it a first-class event for every team that comes: field preparation, certified officials, athletic training, player packets, shade and water at every field. Your support keeps entry affordable for the programs that play, keeps the day free for the families who watch, and backs girls lacrosse at Canyon High School.

Every package below can be adjusted to fit your business, and in-kind support — water, ice, food, swag — is just as welcome.
