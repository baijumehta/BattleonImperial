Graphics · Share That You’re In

Make Your Graphic

Pick a design, add your team photo and logo, and download it ready for Instagram, your team’s stories and the parent group chat. Tag @battleonimperial and we’ll share it.

A logo on a transparent or white background looks best.

Add your images to finish the graphic.

Your photos stay on your device — the graphic is made in your browser and nothing is uploaded to us.

Posting Tips

  • Tag @battleonimperial and use your school’s account so we can reshare it.
  • Use a wide team shot for the “We’re In!” poster; the photo strip is short and wide.
  • Check the parents’ preferences before posting photos of players, as your school’s media policy requires.

Posting Tips

  • Tag @battleonimperial so we can reshare it to the tournament’s families.
  • Use your logo file, not a screenshot — a PNG on a transparent or white background looks sharpest.
  • Post it twice: once when you sign on, and again the week of the tournament.

Posting Tips

  • Tag @battleonimperial so families know to look for your booth.
  • Post the week before with what you’ll be bringing on the day.
  • Use your logo file, not a screenshot, for the sharpest result.
