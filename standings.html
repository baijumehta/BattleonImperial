Round-Robin Pools · Updated Live

Standings

Each pool is a round robin, so every game counts. Standings update as results come in through the day.

Pool games have no overtime, so a tie is recorded as a tie. Ranked on points — three for a win, one for a tie, none for a loss — then goal difference, then goals scored. The full tiebreaker order, including head-to-head, is in the entry packet and governs the official result.

Hosted by the Canyon High School Lacrosse Booster Club, a volunteer parent organization supporting lacrosse at Canyon High School in Anaheim Hills, California.
