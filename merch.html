Good to Know

Merchandise Questions

How does a pre-order work?

Place the order above and you get an email with an order number and the total. Send that amount by Zelle within 7 days with the order number in the memo. We mark it paid, and your gear is waiting at the merchandise table on March 13.

Can you ship it?

No — pickup at the tournament only. It keeps prices where they are.

What if I don’t pay within 7 days?

We may release the order so the stock goes to someone else. Email us if you need a little longer and we’ll hold it.

Can I buy on the day without pre-ordering?

Yes, while stock lasts — the merchandise table takes cash, card and Zelle. Pre-ordering is how you make sure your size is there.

Where does the money go?

Merchandise is sold by the Canyon High School Lacrosse Booster Club, the volunteer parent group that puts the tournament on. Purchases are not tax-deductible donations.
